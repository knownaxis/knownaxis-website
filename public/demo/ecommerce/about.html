The Atelier

Born at the Intersection of Cyber-Aesthetics & Performance

URBANisty was founded to dissolve the boundaries between tactical utility and hyper-modern luxury. We view everyday garments and hardware as ergonomic architecture for the human form.

Each piece is prototyped digitally in 3D dimensional space, stress-tested using algorithmic fluid dynamics, and crafted from high-recycled aerospace composites.
