PERSONAL MANIFESTO

"Design is never just how it looks. It is the frictionless translation of human intent into digital consequence."

Over the past 9 years, I have collaborated with venture-backed founders, engineering leads, and world-class brands to transform unrefined hypotheses into intuitive, enduring digital software and tactile physical products.

My methodology sits at the crossroads of rigorous systems engineering and unapologetic artistic expression. I believe software should not feel sterile; it should possess tactile weight, intentional rhythm, and visceral micro-interactions that leave a lasting imprint on the human consciousness.
